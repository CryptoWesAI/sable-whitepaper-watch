Billing & renewal Immediate charge, 30-day period.
Subscribing to a paid plan debits the full monthly fee from your prepaid balance now and starts a fresh 30-day period.
There is no proration when you switch, and the current period is not refunded.
Cancel keeps you until period end.
Cancelling turns off auto-renew.
Your plan keeps its capabilities until the period ends, then drops to Free.
You manage all of this from the portal.
